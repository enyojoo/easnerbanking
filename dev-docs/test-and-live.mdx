Livemode is the key prefix. There is no `livemode` body flag. In Console, the Test / Live switch in the header is the same split — it applies to Home, Customers, Payments, Accounts, Checkout, and Settings.

| | Test (`easner_sk_test_`) | Live (`easner_sk_live_`) |
|---|---|---|
| Objects | Isolated test objects | Isolated live objects |
| Customer KYC / KYB | Approves immediately. No hosted form | Hosted KYC (person) or KYB (business) on your app. Live bank details and onramp need `approved` |
| Your KYB | Not required to call test `v1` | Complete in Banking → Settings for **access to Easner**, before live |
| Virtual account | Sandbox numbers. No real bank | Named ACH/SEPA after approval. Fiat converts into the vault |
| Wallet address | Token account without verification | Require `approved`. USDC/EURC into the vault |
| Onramp | Hosted page completes and credits the vault | Hosted card onramp after approval, onto the vault |
| Quote | Completes. Send/receive 1:1 | May price wallet destinations |
| Transfer | Create `requires_action`. Confirm debits the **test** vault | Create `requires_action`. Confirm sends from the customer vault. Merchant key cannot confirm |
| Checkout | Test card `4242…`. Nothing charged | Real collect onto your business balance |
| Webhooks | Your test URL | Your live URL |

Test never moves live money. Live never writes test objects.

## Fund a test vault

A new `acct_` is `available: 0`. Before the first send:

1. Console → Accounts → **Simulate test deposit** (session `/api/platform/...`, **not** `/v1`, test only). Credits `type: "deposit"`.
2. Or `POST /v1/accounts/:id/onramp_sessions` and complete the hosted test page. See [Receive](/receive#card-onramp).

Listen for `account.updated` and `transaction.created`.

## Logs and Workbench

Use these in Console Settings while you stay in Test:

| Place | Job |
|---|---|
| Logs | Recent `v1` requests for this business and mode. Open a row for the request. |
| Workbench | Call `v1` as this business without pasting a secret. Retrieve an id you already have. |
| Events | A delivery log — every event, whether or not it reached your URL. Not the catalog; tick events on a webhook endpoint. |

## What you must do before live

<Steps>
  <Step title="Mint a test key">
    Settings → API keys, then Settings → Webhooks. Tick the events you handle. Stay in Test.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run the quickstart">
    [Quickstart](/quickstart): customer → fund test vault → destination → quote → transfer. Confirm `transfer.completed` on the test URL.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive in test">
    [Receive](/receive): verify the customer, fetch sandbox bank details, complete a test onramp. Confirm `account.updated` and `transaction.created`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay with the test card">
    Checkout: register the website, create a test session, pay with `4242`, confirm `checkout.completed`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check Console Logs">
    You should see the `v1` calls. Errors should be gone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete your KYB">
    Banking → Settings → Verification. That is **your** access to Easner, not a `cus_`. Then mint a live key and set a live webhook URL. Tick the same events.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you should not do

<Warning>
  Do not reuse a test key in production. Do not fulfil from the browser `onSuccess` without the webhook. Do not treat Checkout as Receive — a collect does not credit a customer `acct_`.
</Warning>

## Restriction

If the business is restricted for send or deposit, matching writes fail with `permission`.
