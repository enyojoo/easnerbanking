This page is the [Easner Dev Platform](/) introduction.

- **Banking** — accounts, receive, and send on your customer’s `acct_`
- **Checkout** — collect onto **your** business balance (same Connect account as invoices and links)

Use the [quickstart](/quickstart) for a first send, [Receive](/receive) to fund a customer account, and [Checkout](/checkout) to take a payment on your site.
