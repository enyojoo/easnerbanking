Each customer has an **isolated stablecoin vault** (USDC for USD, EURC for EUR, on Solana). Funds sit in that vault — not on Easner’s balance. Easner does not move those funds on a merchant key; the customer authorizes the send from their vault. Virtual-account deposits convert into the vault. Wallet addresses are the vault’s token account.

Checkout is separate: collect onto **your** business balance.
