Send debits an issued customer `acct_`. Create a destination, lock a quote with `source`, then transfer.

<Note>
  **Scope:** `GET` needs `accounts.read`. Creates need `transfers.write`.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Have a funded account">
    The customer’s `acct_` must have `available` ≥ the send amount. Fund it with [Receive](/receive) or keep the first send in test.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a destination">
    Bank, mobile money, wallet, or Easetag. See [Destinations](/destinations).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Quote">
    `POST /v1/quotes` with `source` (required) and `destination`. See [Quotes](/quotes).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Transfer">
    `POST /v1/transfers` with `{ "quote": "qt_…" }` and `Idempotency-Key`. See [Transfers](/transfers).
  </Step>
</Steps>

Fulfil from `transfer.completed` and `transaction.created`. Do not treat Checkout as a source of customer funds — a collect lands on **your** business balance.
