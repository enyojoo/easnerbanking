These are the primitives on `https://api.easner.com/v1`. Combine them. Do not invent a capability the table marks as **Not**.

| Capability | What it is | Not |
|---|---|---|
| **Vault** | Isolated customer vault [`acct_`](/accounts). USD is USDC, EUR is EURC, on Solana. `available` is that vault, in cents. Funds sit with the customer, not on Easner’s books. | Your float. Checkout business balance. Easner taking the funds onto its own books. |
| **Receive** | Credit **that** vault: [virtual account](/receive#virtual-account), [Solana address](/receive#wallet-address), [card onramp](/receive#card-onramp), [Easetag](/receive#easetag). | [Checkout](/checkout) collect. |
| **Send** | Debit the vault to [bank, mobile money, wallet, or Easetag](/send). You create the transfer. The [customer confirms](/transfers#confirm). Corridors: [Payment methods](/payment-methods). | A merchant key moving the vault. HTTP `201` on create meaning the recipient was paid. |
| **Collect** | [Checkout](/checkout) on your website onto **your** business balance. Fulfil from `checkout.completed`. | A credit to a `cus_` `acct_`. |
| **Identify** | **You:** KYB in Banking → Settings for **access to Easner**. **Them:** hosted KYC (person) or KYB (business) on the `cus_` via `POST /v1/customers/:id/verification`, so they onboard on **your** app. | Your KYB approving a `cus_`. Email and name on create. |

## Vault

Creating a [customer](/customers) issues USD. Open EUR with `POST /v1/accounts`. Both currencies sit on the same customer vault owner.

## Receive

Live virtual accounts, live wallet addresses, and onramp need `verification_status = approved` on that `cus_`. Test skips customer verification. Fulfil from `account.updated` and `transaction.created` — not from the HTTP GET that returns bank details.

## Send

`GET /v1/payment_methods` lists rails and corridors. Create a [destination](/destinations), [quote](/quotes) with `source`, then [transfer](/transfers). Status starts `requires_action`. Confirm with `client_secret` (`easner_ta_…`) or open `next_action.url`. See [Transfers](/transfers#confirm).

## Collect

Amount and secret key stay on your server. The browser sees a publishable key and a Checkout `client_secret`. Tick Checkout events on the webhook when you collect.

## Identify

Your KYB is access to Easner. It does not approve a `cus_`. Completing Banking Settings does not set `verification_status`. Put `email` on create — there is no customer `PATCH`.
